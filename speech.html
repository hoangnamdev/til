Getting Started with Azure Speech

1. How to get Azure Speech credentials:

Go to the Azure Portal, create a Speech Service (Free F0 tier is available), navigate to Keys and Endpoint, and copy KEY 1 and the Location/Region.

2. Pronunciation Assessment Details:

This application implements the SpeechSDK.PronunciationAssessmentConfig engine with Phoneme & Syllable level granularity, grading fluency, prosody, completeness, and accuracy.

3. Common issues:

  • 401 Unauthorized: Double check your Speech Key.
  • 400 / 404 Region mismatch: Make sure region matches your resource (e.g., eastus, not East US).
  • Microphone block: Grant microphone access in browser address bar permissions.
